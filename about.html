Experience With a Personal Perspective

Jennifer Thervil is the founder of Bonita Multi-Services LLC, a Bonita Springs-based company created to help individuals, families, property owners and businesses navigate important decisions with responsive, organized and personal support.

Jennifer brings 10+ years of real estate experience and has been licensed in Florida since May 2016. That cross-industry experience shapes a practical, client-first approach focused on understanding the need, explaining the available options clearly and helping clients move toward the appropriate next step.
