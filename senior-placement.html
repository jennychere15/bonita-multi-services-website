Important to Know

Do you determine what level of care someone needs?

No. We do not perform medical assessments, diagnose conditions or determine clinical levels of care. Those decisions should be made with appropriate healthcare professionals and the senior-living provider.

Can families still choose any community they want?

Yes. Families make their own placement decisions. Our role is to help organize and simplify the search process.
