SERVICE SCOPE

Senior Living Coordination From First Conversation to Move-In

A non-medical coordination service for older adults and authorized family contacts who want organized assistance throughout a senior-living search. We help make the process manageable while the resident and family remain in control of their decisions.

Family Intake & Priorities

Discuss preferred location, budget, lifestyle, accessibility, timing and the resident’s wishes. Clinical needs must be identified by qualified healthcare professionals and assessed by the receiving community.

Community Research & Comparison

Organize potential independent-living, assisted-living and memory-care options for family review. Compare published amenities, costs, availability and admission processes, and verify applicable facility licensing through official resources.

Community Tours

Schedule inquiries and up to three local tours within the agreed service area. Accompaniment is included where agreed and within the total service-hour allowance. Families arrange their own transportation.

Application Coordination

Track administrative deadlines, requested paperwork and questions for the community. Residents or authorized representatives supply information and sign their own agreements; medical assessments remain with healthcare professionals.

Move-In Planning

Organize the timeline, community contacts and practical next steps. Moving, packing, transportation and care services are contracted separately with appropriate providers.

Follow-Up

One post-move follow-up to help organize remaining administrative questions and direct the family to the responsible community contact.

SERVICE SCOPE

A Defined Coordination Package

The standard family-paid package covers one resident’s search, up to ten total service hours over sixty days from the initial consultation, and up to three local community tours. Calls, research, emails, tour attendance, agreed travel time and coordination count toward the allowance. Both the time and tour limits apply. Work beyond the allowance or service period is quoted and approved separately.

Before work begins, we confirm the deliverables, included time, schedule, service area and charges in writing. Additional work requires approval. Third-party services have their own contracts, availability and cancellation terms.

The family coordination fee pays for the agreed work and is not contingent on selecting a particular community or on a community paying BMS. The written agreement explains payment milestones, cancellation and any refund of unearned service charges.

If a lawful community compensation arrangement applies, its source and relationship will be disclosed before the family decides. Any separate community compensation does not reduce or reimburse the family coordination fee. Options are not limited to communities that compensate BMS. Community-paid arrangements are subject to a separate agreement and applicable eligibility requirements.

Before We Begin

Who decides on care and admission?

The resident and authorized decision-makers choose the community. Licensed professionals and the community determine clinical suitability, care needs and admission. BMS does not diagnose, assess care levels, administer care, manage medication or guarantee admission, safety or a particular outcome.

Can you help with Medicaid or legal planning?

BMS does not determine Medicaid eligibility, sell insurance, give financial advice, prepare a care plan or provide legal advice. Those questions are directed to qualified professionals. Referral compensation involving Medicaid recipients requires separate eligibility review; we do not promise a community-paid arrangement for such cases.

What is separate from coordination?

Community rent, care charges, assessments, deposits, transportation, moving, legal and financial services are separate. We do not sign for the resident, hold their funds or provide emergency response. Share only non-medical inquiry details through this website; confidential records require an agreed appropriate channel.
