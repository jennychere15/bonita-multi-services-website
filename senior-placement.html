CARE & DISCRETION

A Personal Point of Contact

From the first conversation to move-in planning, we bring continuity to a process that can otherwise feel overwhelming. Our role is to organize, communicate and support your family while keeping the resident’s preferences at the center.

We explain our coordination arrangement and any community compensation relationships before your family makes a decision.

A Few Helpful Details

Who chooses the community?

The resident and authorized family decision-makers choose the community. Healthcare professionals and the receiving community determine care needs and admission suitability; BMS provides non-medical coordination.

Can you help us organize the move?

Yes. We help coordinate the practical timeline, community contacts and connections with moving or other service providers around your family’s plans.
