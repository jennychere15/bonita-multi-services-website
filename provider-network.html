VENDOR PAID APPOINTMENT COORDINATION

Agree the Fee. Prepay. Receive the Introduction.

BMS presents a service opportunity matched to your trade and location. You review the request, agree the appointment details and exact coordination fee, and pay BMS before the confirmed customer introduction. You may decline an opportunity before accepting it.

Routine appointment coordination$35 per accepted request
Additional scheduling or request coordination$55 per accepted request

The exact fee and included coordination are disclosed before acceptance and payment. BMS collects only its coordination fee; you collect the service payment directly from your customer. A sale, customer payment or ongoing job volume is not guaranteed.

A fresh BMS-coordinated request requires a fresh accepted fee, even when it involves the same vendor and customer. Rescheduling the same referral does not create a new fee. Future work arranged directly between the parties is outside this fee obligation.

Fee earning, cancellations and any refund or credit terms are disclosed in the vendor agreement and booking acceptance before payment. Submission of an application does not authorize charges. Provider participation is subject to approval and applicable trade requirements. This fee program does not include legal, medical, financial, insurance, mortgage, title or real estate referrals.
