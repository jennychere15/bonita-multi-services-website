HOW THE NETWORK WORKS

Performance-Based Referral Structure

BMS does not charge providers for unconverted introductions. Referral fees are due only after a BMS-generated opportunity results in collected revenue, unless a category is subject to different legal or professional restrictions.

Completed job under $500$35 BMS referral fee
$500-$1,499$75
$1,500-$4,999$150
$5,000+5%, capped at $500
Recurring service contract10% of first month's collected revenue

Certain regulated professions or transactions may require a different compensation structure or prohibit referral compensation. BMS may waive or modify fees where required by law, licensing rules, or written provider agreement. Participation never guarantees leads.
