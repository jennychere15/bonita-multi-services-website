SERVICE SCOPE

An Organized Plan for Your Southwest Florida Move

Practical, non-medical planning and administrative coordination for a household moving to or within Southwest Florida. We begin with your timing, priorities, budget and responsibilities, then build a clear checklist and coordinate the tasks you authorize.

Move Planning

An initial sixty-minute intake consultation and a personalized moving checklist and timeline for one household.

Service Provider Coordination

Research and coordinate up to three service providers, such as movers, cleaners or storage businesses. You approve provider selection and contract directly for their work.

Utility Scheduling Assistance

Help organize utility or internet inquiries and appointment scheduling. The client opens accounts, authorizes deposits and supplies confidential credentials directly to the provider.

Local Orientation

Organize objective information about government services, schools, libraries and daily conveniences using your preferences. We do not steer housing choices based on protected characteristics.

Housing & Lodging Pathways

Connect your needs to an appropriate real estate or corporate-lodging service. Licensed property searches, showings and lease or purchase negotiations are separate Realty Hub brokerage work.

Follow-Up

One follow-up call to check the administrative checklist and organize remaining next steps.

SERVICE SCOPE

Five Hours of Focused Coordination

The standard package includes up to five total service hours over thirty days from the initial consultation, including the intake and follow-up calls. Research, calls, emails and scheduling count toward the allowance. Provider count and hour limits both apply. Additional hours, on-site visits, travel or extended projects require a separately approved scope.

Before work begins, we confirm the deliverables, included time, schedule, service area and charges in writing. Additional work requires approval. Third-party services have their own contracts, availability and cancellation terms.

Movers, packing, storage, cleaning, lodging, transportation, deposits and utility bills are separate. BMS is not a moving carrier and does not physically pack, transport goods, provide personal care or guarantee provider performance. Business and complex relocations are quoted individually.

Before We Begin

Is buying or renting a home included?

No. Real estate services are provided separately by Jennifer Thervil, Florida real estate sales associate, through Realty Hub. A relocation package does not create a brokerage relationship or include a home purchase, lease negotiation, legal advice or immigration assistance.
