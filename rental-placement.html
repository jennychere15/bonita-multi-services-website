SERVICE SCOPE

Property Readiness and Licensed Rental Placement

Support for owners considering short-term, vacation, furnished or long-term rental use. Begin with your property, intended use, availability and goals so the right administrative or brokerage scope can be established.

Owner Intake & Readiness

Discuss furnishings, amenities, access, upkeep, photography needs and operational contacts. Organize a readiness checklist using the owner’s priorities.

Launch Coordination

Coordinate approved photography, cleaning, furnishing or maintenance requests with independent providers. Providers contract directly for their work and required credentials are reviewed where applicable.

Licensed Rental Placement

Rental listings, tenant or guest procurement, showings and lease negotiation requiring a real estate license are handled by Jennifer Thervil, Florida real estate sales associate, through Realty Hub under a separate brokerage agreement.

Management Provider Introductions

If ongoing operations are needed, discuss appropriate independent management providers. Their licensing, contracts, charges and availability must be confirmed separately.

SERVICE SCOPE

A Property-Specific Written Scope

A consultation establishes which readiness tasks are needed, who is responsible and what the agreed deliverables and timeline will be. Administrative coordination and licensed brokerage work are documented separately.

Before work begins, we confirm the deliverables, included time, schedule, service area and charges in writing. Additional work requires approval. Third-party services have their own contracts, availability and cancellation terms.

Owners must confirm permitted rental use, association restrictions, local zoning and business requirements, applicable lodging licensing, taxes and insurance with the responsible authorities and professionals before launch. BMS does not guarantee approval, occupancy, rental revenue or investment performance.

Full-service property management, rent collection, escrow handling, guest operations, maintenance performance and tax remittance are not included in BMS’s readiness package. Any separately authorized management or brokerage service requires its own appropriate agreement.

Before We Begin

Can you prepare or advise on my lease?

Jennifer’s authorized brokerage activity is handled through Realty Hub. BMS’s clerical service does not select legal forms or provide lease drafting, legal interpretation or legal advice. An attorney should address legal questions.

Does this cover ongoing rental management?

No. Ongoing management is a separate service provided by an appropriately authorized business. The property owner chooses and contracts with that provider.
