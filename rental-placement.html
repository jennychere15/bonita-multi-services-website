A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Good questions. Clear answers.

Do you work with both owners and renters?

Yes. Owners can submit a property for leasing, rental placement, readiness or management review. Renters can share their search criteria for short-term, seasonal, furnished or annual homes. We confirm scope and current options personally.

Can I browse available rentals here?

Start with the rental search brief below. We will discuss current options that fit your dates, budget and location preferences. This page is not a live inventory or MLS feed; submitting a search does not reserve a home.

Can you help with property management?

Yes—start by requesting a management proposal. We review the property, rental arrangement and operating needs, then identify the appropriate service arrangement. Licensed leasing and brokerage activities are handled by Jennifer Thervil under the applicable brokerage agreement. Ongoing management is subject to a separate written agreement with the identified provider; BMS coordination and independent-provider work are scoped separately.

What is the difference between seasonal and annual rentals?

A seasonal search usually begins with a defined arrival and departure window and a furnished-home preference. An annual search usually focuses on a longer lease and everyday living needs. Exact lease length, furnishings, utilities, association rules and costs are confirmed for each home.

What does it cost to place a property or find a rental?

Fees, representation, owner services and any renter-side charges are discussed before an engagement. Property rent, deposits, association applications, utilities, taxes and other charges vary by property and lease. No fee or payment is collected through these inquiry forms.

Is any home eligible for short-term rentals?

Eligibility depends on the exact jurisdiction, zoning, association documents, licensing and the proposed use. We start with the address and intended rental term. Use the official owner resources below and verify the requirements for the specific property before advertising or committing to a stay.

How should I compare schools and neighborhoods?

Use the same official district and community resources for every address you are considering. Verify school assignment, eligibility and enrollment directly with the district; location alone does not guarantee placement. Tell us your objective needs such as commute, amenities and home features.

What happens after I send an inquiry?

We review your property or rental-search brief, contact you by your chosen method and discuss next steps. Listing, representation, screening, management or reservation arrangements are documented separately. Do not send funds until the property, authorized party and written terms have been confirmed.
