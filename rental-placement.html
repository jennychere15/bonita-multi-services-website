OWNER FAQ

Rental Property Placement FAQ

What is vacation rental placement?

Placement is designed for owners who need help preparing and positioning a furnished property for the vacation-rental market and coordinating the agreed launch process.

Do you provide ongoing property management?

No. BMS focuses on rental property placement. If you need ongoing property management, we can discuss connecting you with an appropriate independent provider through the BMS Preferred Service Provider Network.

Do you work with properties that have never been vacation rentals?

Yes. The consultation is designed to assess current readiness, owner objectives and appropriate next steps.

Which areas do you serve?

Our Southwest Florida focus includes Bonita Springs, Naples, Estero, Fort Myers, Cape Coral and surrounding communities, subject to property and service availability.
