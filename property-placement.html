A few helpful details

Can you work alongside my property management company?

Yes. We can discuss placement, prospect follow-up and marketing support within your authority and existing agreements. Scope and responsibilities are confirmed first.

Do you provide ongoing management?

This service focuses on placement and marketing. Ongoing management is outside this campaign’s scope.

What are the fees?

Fees depend on the property and the agreed services and are disclosed in writing before engagement. Rent, deposits, association charges, government fees and third-party costs are separate where applicable.

Can a property already listed with another broker be included?

We first clarify existing representation and any required broker-to-broker cooperation. Submitting a property does not change an existing agreement.
