Yes. Mobile appointments are available by appointment throughout the service area, subject to availability and travel fees.

Can you notarize online?

Remote online notarization is available for eligible transactions and signers through an approved platform.

No. We can perform permitted notarial and administrative services, but we cannot select legal documents or provide legal advice.
