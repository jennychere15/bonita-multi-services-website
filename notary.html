What an apostille does

An apostille certifies the origin of a public document for use under the Hague Apostille Convention. It is different from notarization and does not approve the document’s contents. For other destinations, authentication or additional legalization may be required.

What our team coordinates

Document origin and destination review, submission checklists, required original or certified records, agreed handling, tracking and return delivery. We explain the BMS facilitation fee separately from government fees and shipping.

Florida documents follow the Florida authority’s requirements. Another state’s records or federal documents follow the appropriate issuing authority’s route. We confirm that path before submission.

What is the difference between notarization and an apostille?

A notarization concerns a specific notarial act, such as taking an acknowledgment or administering an oath. An apostille authenticates the origin of a public document for use under the Hague Apostille Convention. It does not certify the truth of the document’s contents. Tell us the issuing jurisdiction and destination country so we can help organize the appropriate route.

Can I use online notarization from another state?

Eligible signers can meet our Florida online notary while located elsewhere in the United States. We confirm document suitability, identification, technology, witness arrangements and the recipient’s acceptance before scheduling. Some transactions require a different signing method.

Should I sign before my appointment?

Leave the document unsigned unless our team confirms otherwise for the required act. An oath or affirmation with a signature generally needs signing before the notary. If you do not know which notarial act is needed, ask the document recipient or your attorney to specify it.

What identification should I bring?

Have a current government-issued photo ID ready and tell us about any name differences before the appointment. Our team will confirm whether your identification meets the applicable Florida requirements or whether another legally permitted identification method is available. For online appointments, additional verification steps apply.

Can you notarize or certify a birth certificate?

A Florida notary cannot make an attested photocopy of a vital record when a certified copy is available from its official custodian. Birth, death, marriage and court records usually need the appropriate government-issued certified document for apostille processing. We help identify the relevant office and submission instructions.

How long does an apostille take?

Timing depends on document readiness, the issuing authority, its current workload and shipping. We discuss the available submission method and estimated timeline after review. A requested deadline is not a government processing guarantee.

Do law firms and companies have their own portal?

Invited individual and business contacts use the private BMS client portal to view their assigned requests, milestones, messages and payment requests. Access follows the invited email address; company-wide employee access, shared inbox permissions and document uploads are arranged separately rather than assumed.

How are fees and payments handled?

We confirm a written service quote before you pay. It separates the notarial act from any agreed travel, signing coordination, witnesses, apostille handling, government charges and shipping. Existing clients can open an issued payment request in their private portal; new clients begin with a service request.

Can I send confidential documents through the request form?

Use the form for scheduling and general document details only. Do not enter Social Security numbers, ID images, financial information, medical details or confidential case documents. We coordinate an appropriate document exchange after reviewing the request.

Are appointments available throughout Florida?

Our team coordinates office, mobile and online options. In-person appointments throughout Florida depend on local notary availability and your request. Remote appointments serve eligible signers nationwide. Same-day, evening and weekend appointments are confirmed individually.
