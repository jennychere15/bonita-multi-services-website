SERVICE SCOPE

Notary and Signing Appointments

Appointment-based signing support through a commissioned Florida notary, with in-office, mobile and remote online options accepted according to the document, signer, location and schedule.

In-Office & Mobile

Arrange an appointment at the Bonita Springs office or an agreed home, business, hospital or senior-community location. Office visits and mobile travel are confirmed individually.

Notarial Acts

Eligible acknowledgments, oaths or affirmations, and permitted copy attestations. The signer appears personally, provides satisfactory identification and signs willingly under the applicable procedure.

Remote Online Notarization

Eligible transactions use compliant audio-video technology and identity verification through the selected platform. The Florida online notary remains physically in Florida; signer location and document eligibility are checked before acceptance.

Loan & Estate Signing Support

Coordinate execution of documents supplied by the client, lender, title company or attorney. Signing support does not include legal explanations, loan advice or preparing estate-planning documents.

Witness Coordination

Discuss document-specific witness requirements, availability and separate arrangements before the appointment. Witnesses and execution requirements are not assumed to be interchangeable across documents.

Employer-Authorized I-9 Assistance

Selected Form I-9 assistance only when the employer designates an authorized representative and the task is permitted. This is not notarization and no notary seal is applied to the form.

SERVICE SCOPE

Apostille Facilitation

Administrative coordination for an eligible document issued or notarized in Florida, based on its origin, issuing official and destination country. We review the published submission requirements, organize the request, coordinate delivery to the appropriate authority and track return handling. The government authority issues the apostille or authentication; BMS does not issue one.

The standard first-document package includes the applicable Florida apostille filing charge and standard tracked shipping to the state and back to one U.S. address. Additional eligible documents may be submitted in the same shipment under a separately agreed add-on. The unit is a document requiring its own certification, not simply a page count.

Translations, international delivery, expedited private couriers, notarization and obtaining original certified copies are quoted separately. Federal and out-of-state documents require their respective authority and a separate review. Processing depends on the issuing authority, document condition and carrier; no government turnaround or overseas acceptance is guaranteed.

SERVICE SCOPE

Prepare for Your Appointment

Tell us the document type, number of signers, requested location and timing without sending confidential contents through the public form. Confirm acceptable identification, document completeness, witness arrangements and any required original or certified record. The document recipient or attorney should identify the required notarial act; the notary does not choose legal forms or advise which document you need.

Notarial charges comply with applicable Florida limits. Any separate travel, administrative, platform or coordination charges are explained and agreed before service. A notarial act may be declined when statutory requirements are not met. Notarization verifies the required signing act; it does not establish a document’s legal effect.

We are not attorneys and do not provide legal advice or representation, immigration advice or clinical capacity assessments. Certified copies of vital or public records must come from the authorized custodian when required.

Before We Begin

Can every signer use online notarization?

No. We confirm signer location, identification, technology access, document restrictions and any special witness requirements before accepting a remote appointment. An online booking request does not guarantee eligibility.

Do you arrange an apostille before reviewing the document?

No. Confirm the issuing jurisdiction, original or certified-copy requirements and destination country before paying. Apostille and consular legalization are different processes; the correct route must be identified for the document.
