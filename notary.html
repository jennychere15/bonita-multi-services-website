COMMON QUESTIONS

Southwest Florida Notary FAQ

Do you offer a mobile notary in Bonita Springs and nearby areas?

Yes. Mobile appointments are available by appointment in Bonita Springs and surrounding Southwest Florida areas, subject to availability and travel fees.

Can you notarize online?

Remote online notarization is available for eligible transactions and signers through an approved platform.

Do you help with apostilles?

Apostille facilitation is available as an administrative service for eligible documents. Requirements vary based on the document and destination.

Can you tell me which legal document or form I need?

No. We can perform permitted notarial and administrative services, but we cannot select legal documents or provide legal advice.
