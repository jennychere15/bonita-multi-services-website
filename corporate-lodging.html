One Request or Ongoing Coordination

The monthly plan includes up to four lodging requests and eight total service hours per billing month. Both limits apply. Each request covers one destination and one set of dates; group and multiple-property work is quoted separately. Calls, research, emails and follow-up count toward the allowance. Unused hours and requests do not roll over.

Support is during agreed business hours. Additional work requires written approval. Monthly renewal, cancellation, start date and billing terms are provided before enrollment. A consultation or inquiry does not activate a subscription.

Before work begins, we confirm the deliverables, included time, schedule, service area and charges in writing. Additional work requires approval. Third-party services have their own contracts, availability and cancellation terms.

Accommodation charges, taxes, deposits, provider cancellation fees and third-party expenses are paid separately to the responsible provider. BMS does not hold guest deposits or promise particular rates, availability or savings.

Before We Begin

How are reservations and leases handled?

Accommodation reservations are made and confirmed by the client and an appropriately authorized lodging or travel provider. Any activity requiring travel registration or an exemption is accepted only after the applicable authorization is confirmed. Residential or commercial property searches, leasing and negotiations requiring a real estate license are handled by Jennifer Thervil, Florida real estate sales associate, through Realty Hub under a separate brokerage scope.

Is this a hotel or property management service?

BMS coordinates the agreed administrative work. The accommodation operator provides lodging, maintenance, refunds and guest services under its contract. This package does not include full-service property management, flights, transportation or around-the-clock emergency support.
