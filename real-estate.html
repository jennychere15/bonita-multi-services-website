Before We Begin

Do you provide rental placement?

Rental searches, listing, procuring tenants and lease negotiation requiring a license are handled through Realty Hub. See Rental Property Placement for the distinction between brokerage and BMS’s property-readiness coordination.

What areas and property types are available?

The focus is Southwest Florida, including Bonita Springs, Naples, Estero, Fort Myers and Cape Coral. Each request is accepted based on the property, availability and brokerage authorization. Statewide requests are discussed individually; partner coverage is confirmed before commitment.

Is an inquiry a representation agreement?

No. An inquiry or consultation starts the conversation. Any representation and brokerage compensation are documented separately. Services are offered consistent with fair housing and equal opportunity requirements.
