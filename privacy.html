Privacy & Service Requests

Information you choose to provide

Service requests and vendor applications ask for contact details, service or business information, location, availability and the details needed to review your request. Some service request and vendor application forms use FormSubmit to process and forward the information you choose to provide to Admin@bonitamultiservices.com. This may include contact details, service preferences, locations, timing, property information and vendor business information. FormSubmit states that it retains submissions for 30 days. Submitting a service request does not confirm an appointment.

Notary network subscriptions and client portals

Notary, apostille, and business and sales consulting inquiries are sent to the BMS backend hosted on Render, stored as project records and used to respond to your request. Service and project records may include your contact details, company, location, requested appointments, service scope, payment status and commercial plan usage. Notary network applications also include commission details, expiry, service areas, capabilities and verification records. Separate private client and vendor sessions control access to their assigned records. Commercial billing uses Stripe subscriptions; mobile vendor payments use connected accounts with the agreed revenue share. Stripe collects card and payment-onboarding information through its own hosted process. Transactional notifications are delivered through our email provider, Resend. Invited clients sign in through a single-use email link valid for 15 minutes; an HttpOnly session cookie can keep the portal signed in for up to seven days. Sign out on shared devices. Issued BMS service payment links open Stripe Checkout; card information is entered on Stripe, not in the project portal. Keep sensitive identity, tax, financial and personnel records out of inquiry forms and portal messages.

How we use and share it

BMS uses request information to contact you, review the request, coordinate service and maintain appointment and business records. With your authorization, necessary contact, property and job details are shared with the selected independent provider. Vendor application information is used to evaluate participation and coordinate appropriate opportunities. Request consent does not authorize unrelated marketing.

Wedding inquiries and provider applications

Wedding service requests and wedding-provider applications use FormSubmit to forward your contact information, preferences and optional attachments to BMS by email. Optional attachments are limited to vows, ceremony outlines, non-sensitive planning documents and public provider brochures or portfolio samples. FormSubmit states it retains text submissions for 30 days; uploaded files are not available in its archive. BMS receives files in its business email and uses them to review and coordinate your request. Contact BMS to request correction or deletion. Do not submit identity documents, signed licenses, Social Security numbers, tax forms, banking details or confidential records. Request an appropriate sharing method if those records are needed.

Documents and payments

Do not enter payment details, Social Security numbers, identity documents, medical information or confidential files in these forms or ordinary email. Contact BMS to arrange an appropriate method when documents are needed. This website does not collect card payments through its request forms.

Appointment requests across BMS services

The common appointment form requires a service selection and brief description before opening the next scheduling step. With JavaScript enabled, requests are sent to the BMS backend hosted on Render and include the contact information, selected service and non-sensitive service description you provide. The form has a FormSubmit fallback when JavaScript is unavailable. Some specialized service forms continue through their existing service-specific submission routes. Switching service departments may temporarily store your contact fields and brief in your browser’s session storage to prefill the next BMS form; that handoff is removed when read and is ignored after 30 minutes. Scheduling then takes place in Google Calendar or the applicable BMS notary or room booking flow. An inquiry does not itself reserve an appointment.

External services

Booking links open Google Calendar. Website form submissions are processed by FormSubmit, which uses spam-prevention services such as reCAPTCHA. Background photographs are loaded from an external image provider. Your email app and external websites operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

Advertising measurement

This website uses the Google tag for Google Ads measurement. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to measure visits and advertising interactions and support advertising features. The form fields you submit are not explicitly sent to Google by this tag installation. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Questions and record requests

To ask about information you provided, request a correction or discuss deletion of records, email Admin@bonitamultiservices.com. Some records may need to be retained for business, transaction or applicable legal requirements.
