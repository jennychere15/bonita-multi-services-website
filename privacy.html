Privacy & Service Requests

Information you choose to provide

Service requests and vendor applications ask for contact details, service or business information, location, availability and the details needed to review your request. The forms prepare an email draft in your email app. BMS receives the information only when you send the email or contact us directly.

How we use and share it

BMS uses request information to contact you, review the request, coordinate service and maintain appointment and business records. With your authorization, necessary contact, property and job details are shared with the selected independent provider. Vendor application information is used to evaluate participation and coordinate appropriate opportunities. Request consent does not authorize unrelated marketing.

Documents and payments

Do not enter payment details, Social Security numbers, identity documents, medical information or confidential files in these forms or ordinary email. Contact BMS to arrange an appropriate method when documents are needed. This website does not collect card payments through its request forms.

External services

Booking links open Google Calendar. Background photographs are loaded from an external image provider. Your email app and external websites operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

Questions and record requests

To ask about information you provided, request a correction or discuss deletion of records, email Admin@bonitamultiservices.com. Some records may need to be retained for business, transaction or applicable legal requirements.
