GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

Before we begin.

What does the full Business Setup & Launch package cover?

Your proposal selects the work required for your business: formation and EIN coordination, location and approvals tracking, website and offer development, staffing preparation, and opening support. Every engagement identifies deliverables, included hours, responsibilities, milestone payments and exclusions before work begins.

Why does a full launch range from $10,000 to $25,000 or more?

The number of locations, business model, agency requirements, staffing, website scope and implementation hours determine the fee. A straightforward launch may begin at $10,000. More complex or time-intensive work is quoted at $25,000 or above. These are consulting and coordination fees, not an all-inclusive budget to open the business.

Can you help with commercial space and negotiations?

Yes. We can develop your location requirements and coordinate commercial property search and negotiations through Jennifer Thervil, Florida Licensed Real Estate Sales Associate with Realty Hub. Real estate representation and compensation are handled under a separate brokerage agreement.

Do you obtain government approvals or make legal and tax decisions?

BMS organizes requirements, coordinates applications and tracks agency follow-up and inspections. Agencies determine approvals and timing. You select your business structure and tax treatment with your attorney or CPA when needed. Formation support is administrative; EIN assistance requires authorization. We do not guarantee licensing, zoning or inspection approval.

Are government fees, buildout and advertising included?

No. Filing and license fees, rent, deposits, construction, furnishings, insurance, third-party professionals, software subscriptions, domains, hosting, advertising and event/vendor costs are separate unless your written scope expressly includes a specific item. The IRS does not charge for an EIN; our listed fee is for optional assistance.

How do booking and payments work?

Book an introductory conversation or submit your project brief. We then confirm scope and timing. Your customized Stripe payment request is issued for the agreed work or milestone. A 90-minute executive strategy session is $350 and is scheduled after coordination and payment. Monthly support is agreed separately; it is not started automatically by an inquiry.

What can I do in the client portal?

Once invited by BMS, use your private email sign-in link to see your project scope, milestones, messages and payment requests. Your project is visible only to your invited email account. Payment information is entered in Stripe, not in portal messages.

Do you work throughout Florida?

Yes. Business and sales consulting is available statewide through remote collaboration and scheduled on-site engagements. Travel and on-site time are quoted in advance. Approval requirements depend on your exact address, jurisdiction and industry.

Can you guarantee sales growth or an opening date?

We build a practical strategy, implementation process and measurable review cadence. Revenue, hiring outcomes and government processing times depend on factors beyond our control, so sales results and opening dates are not guaranteed.
