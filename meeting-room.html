PLAN YOUR MEETING

Your Bonita Springs meeting space, made simple.

Where is the meeting room located?

Find Bonita Multi-Services at Oak Creek Crossing, 11100 Bonita Beach Road, Unit 108-B-1&2, Bonita Springs, FL 34135. A convenient meeting point for professionals based in Bonita Springs, Estero and Naples.

What is included with my room rental?

Exclusive use of the private room for up to six guests, guest Wi-Fi, a presentation TV and a whiteboard. Coffee service, breakfast catering, printing and scanning can be arranged separately.

Can I rent the room by the hour or for a full day?

Yes. Hourly rentals are $45 per hour with a two-hour minimum. Full-day rental is $165 for 6 AM–6 PM Eastern. Reservations are available seven days a week, beginning November 15, 2026.
